How it works

  • Your coins are added to your account straight after the payment goes through, usually within a minute.

Coins

  • Coins and items belong to the account that bought them.
  • We may change what things cost in the game, or add and remove items, as the game grows.

Refunds

  • If you paid and your coins did not arrive, contact us and we will add the coins or refund you.
  • If you were charged by mistake, or charged twice, we will refund you.
  • We do not give refunds because you changed your mind, or for coins you have already spent.
  • Nothing here takes away your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

To ask for a refund, email us with the Google account you play on and the date of the payment.

Playing fair

Do not cheat, use scripts or bots, or abuse bugs to get coins or items. Accounts that do may have their coins and items reset, or be removed from the game. One account can be used on one device at a time.

Under 18

If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before you buy anything.

Your information

When you sign in with Google we keep your account ID, your display name and picture, and your game progress (coins, items, worlds, friends). Stripe handles the payment and keeps its own record of it. We do not sell your information.
